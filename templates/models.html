Fusion

Each model is judged against its own threshold. Then fused = 0.5·pCNN + 0.5·pclinical, compared with the matching fused threshold 0.5·{{ cfg.CNN_THRESHOLD }} + 0.5·{{ status.clinical.threshold }}. Disagreement between models is never hidden by the average: it always has high uncertainty, and is labelled TB possible (fused probability above the fused threshold) or Indeterminate.

Why fusion has no test metrics yet: the X-ray images and the clinical records come from different, unpaired datasets, so no patient has both. Fusion weights and fused performance can only be estimated from real paired cases. The app stores confirmed outcomes for exactly this purpose (see scripts/evaluate_fusion.py).
